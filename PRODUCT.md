# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, deployed by GitHub Pages from `main` (custom domain xanadu.ahmads.build). Whether to move to a framework when the extra pages arrive is undecided; the stack question was asked and not yet answered.

## Users

- **Primary:** C-suite and transformation leads at enterprises and government bodies in the UAE and wider GCC: CEO, COO, CDO, Chief AI Officer. They have a mandate and a budget, little time, and low tolerance for hype. They often read on a phone between meetings.
- **Secondary:** government and university decision makers (existing public clients include Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence, American University of Ras Al Khaimah, Government of Ajman, Government of Sharjah); marketing and brand leaders arriving through Canva.
- **Market priority (next 12 months):** UAE / GCC first; UK (London) second.

## Product Purpose

Xanadu takes enterprises from AI experiments to AI that runs the business. **AI transformation (Claude, agents, workflow automation) is the core offer; Canva is a supporting creative and communications layer** and an easy way in. The site will become the real xanadu.co. Success: a qualified buyer takes the AI readiness assessment, then books a conversation.

## Positioning

Undecided which single claim leads (asked, not yet answered). Confirmed facts it can draw on:
- in-region, in-person execution, as opposed to fly-in consultancies and software-only labs;
- the CEO built the largest B2B partner business on Meta's platform and one of the earliest on Google's;
- Certified Services Partner in the Claude Partner Network, and an authorised Canva reseller and services partner;
- fixed-fee, fixed-scope steps that reach production.

## Operating Context

- **Method:** Assess (free) → Blueprint (paid plan: costed roadmap and business case) → Enable (go-live, adoption to target) → Govern (brand, licence, cost, policy, admin; sold on its own too) → Build (integrations and automation into production). Executive AI Coaching runs alongside any step.
- **Sectors:** Real Estate, Higher Education, QSR & Franchise; selectively Retail, Hospitality, Financial Services, Public Sector, Transport & Logistics.
- **Offices:** Gate Avenue, DIFC, Dubai (HQ); Hoxton Square, London.
- **Contact:** hello@xanadu.co.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Primary action:** the AI readiness assessment. Booking a call comes after it.
- **Pages needed:** Home, AI Transformation, About / Team.
- **Language:** English site with Arabic accents only (no full right-to-left version). Arabic copy needs a native speaker's check before launch.
- Undecided: whether the assessment captures leads; founder and team names, photos and bios.

## Brand Commitments

- **Logo:** the Xanadu ring-and-X mark and lowercase wordmark keep their original shapes. Recolouring is allowed; reshaping is not. Brand anchors are the logo's teal and orange.
- **Voice:** plain, executive, declarative; outcomes and control; no hype words.
- **The owner rejects:** the old xanadu.co look (dated serif headlines, mint cards, template feel, stock AI imagery such as the rainbow brain) and the first concept's dark, techy, generic grid-and-glow look, which had no local character.
- **Feel references (for feel, never for copying):** Anthropic for warmth and calm confidence; Palantir / Scale AI for institutional, government-grade weight.

## Evidence on Hand

- Forrester Total Economic Impact study of Canva Enterprise (commissioned by Canva, 2026): 414% ROI, 50% lower agency spend, 75% fewer routine design requests, payback under 6 months. Always attributed; it is Canva's proof, not Xanadu's.
- Public client names listed above (already on xanadu.co).
- Founder track record (Meta, Google), named in principle; the founder's own name and photo are not yet supplied.
- **Absent and never to be fabricated:** Xanadu case studies, outcome metrics, testimonials, client quotes, seat counts, deployment figures. Any illustrative demonstration must be labelled as an example.

## Product Principles

1. AI transformation leads every page; Canva supports it.
2. Every path ends at the readiness assessment.
3. Signal four things at once: frontier, enterprise-safe, local, proven. No one of them can be traded away for another.
4. Show only what is true: label demonstrations, attribute borrowed proof, invent nothing.
5. Gulf first: Dubai before London, and regional clients and Arabic visible.
