# Xanadu: Design Brief

Captured from the brand interview on 2026-11-16. Every design and content decision on this site should trace back to this file.

## 1. Business

- **Core offer:** **AI transformation comes first.** Claude, agents and workflow automation are the headline. Canva is the supporting creative and communications layer and an easy way in, not an equal pillar.
- **Model:** Assess (free) → Blueprint → Enable → Govern → Build. Fixed fee, fixed scope, sold one step at a time.
- **Market priority (next 12 months):** **UAE / GCC first.** The UK (London) is secondary.
- **Role of this site:** it **will become the real xanadu.co**. It is not a throwaway concept, so build it to production standard.

## 2. Audience

- **Primary buyer:** **C-suite and transformation leads**: CEO, COO, CDO, Chief AI Officer. They have a mandate and a budget, little time, and low tolerance for hype.
- Secondary: government and university leaders (current clients include MBZUAI, AURAK, the Government of Ajman and the Government of Sharjah), plus marketing and brand leaders coming in through Canva.

## 3. What the site must achieve

- **Primary action: take the AI readiness assessment.** It is the main call to action in the hero, the nav and the closing section, and every path leads to it. Booking a call comes second, after the assessment.
- **First 5 seconds, the visitor should feel all four:**
  1. **Frontier.** These people are ahead of the curve.
  2. **Enterprise-safe.** Serious enough for government, security and governance.
  3. **Local.** In-region and in person, not a fly-in consultancy.
  4. **Proven operators.** The team has scaled platform businesses before (Meta, Google).
- **Pages:** Home · **AI Transformation** (service deep dive) · **About / Team**. Canva stays a section, not its own page, for now.

## 4. Proof: current constraints

- **No client case studies or outcome numbers are available yet.**
- Allowed: Canva/Forrester platform stats (always attributed), client names already public on xanadu.co, and the founder's track record.
- Do **not** invent metrics, testimonials or case studies. Use clearly marked placeholders where real proof will go later.
- The hero "deployment console" must read as an *illustrative example*, never as a real client result.

## 5. What we don't like

**Current xanadu.co**
- Dated and generic: serif headlines, mint cards, a WordPress-template feel.
- Stock AI imagery: the rainbow brain and similar clichés.

**First concept (v1, dark)**
- **Too dark and too techy.** It reads like a developer-tool startup, not something a government CIO trusts.
- **Not distinctive.** The grid, glow and mono look is what every AI startup ships.
- **Not local.** Nothing signals the UAE or GCC.

## 6. References

- **Anthropic:** warm, editorial, calm confidence, generous whitespace, human.
- **Palantir / Scale AI:** institutional weight and government credibility; serious, restrained, precise.
- In short: *warm authority*. Calm and confident, never loud.

## 7. Direction for v2 (derived)

| Area | Direction |
|---|---|
| **Mode** | **Light-first.** Warm paper and sand backgrounds as the default. Ink is used sparingly, for weight (one or two sections at most). No grid-and-glow. |
| **Logo** | Unchanged shapes. Teal ring, orange X. A dark-ink wordmark on light backgrounds. |
| **Colour** | **Pinned (round 2):** the sharper v1 teal **#00A3B4** and signal orange **#FF6B1A**, on light grounds. Not the original xanadu.co petrol teal and amber. No black or near-black page backgrounds. |
| **Type** | A confident, refined sans for the main typeface and an editorial companion for big statements. Avoid the old DM Serif look and the v1 everything-mono look; mono only for small data labels, if at all. |
| **Local character** | Dubai (DIFC) shown prominently, regional client names, Gulf-first copy. No Arabic for now (owner's decision, round 2). |
| **Imagery** | No stock AI imagery. Use real people and real places once available; until then use typography, geometry and plain product-like diagrams. |
| **Tone of voice** | Plain, executive and declarative. Short sentences. No hype words ("revolutionary", "unleash"). Talk about outcomes and control. |
| **Interaction** | Restrained motion. The assessment is the one rich interactive element and should feel premium. |
| **Arabic scope** | **None for now.** English only; revisit accents later with a native speaker. |

## 8. Open questions

- Founder and team names, photos and bios for the About page.
- When real case studies or anonymised outcomes will be available.
- Whether the assessment should capture leads (email gate) or stay ungated.
- Final Arabic copy, to be checked by a native speaker.
